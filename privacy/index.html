Privacy Notice

This page explains what information this website receives and how it is used.

Information you provide

The contact form collects your name, email address, selected interest, message, and a submission timestamp. This information is used to respond to your inquiry and manage the resulting professional conversation.

Website analytics

Google Analytics 4 is used to understand website use and interactions such as booking-link clicks. Analytics events do not intentionally include the contents of your inquiry or your contact details.

Service providers

Formspree processes contact-form submissions. Google Calendar handles booking activity after you follow an external booking link. Those services process information under their own privacy terms.

Retention and sharing

Inquiry information is kept only as long as reasonably necessary for communication, record-keeping, and applicable obligations. It is not sold. It may be processed by the service providers needed to operate the website and respond to you.

Your choices and contact

You can avoid submitting the contact form or using the booking link. To ask about, correct, or request deletion of information you submitted, email b.beysuk@gmail.com.

This notice describes current website practices and is not legal advice.
