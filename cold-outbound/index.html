Diagnosis

Most companies' outbound is assembled, not engineered.

Before any build, I look at what's already there. The same three faults show up in almost every company I audit.

01

A founder doing outbound part-time

The person best placed to close deals is the one sending 20 cold emails a week between everything else. It never gets the volume or consistency to compound.

02

Messaging that reads like every other competitor

Generic pitches land in the same inbox as fifty identical ones. No ICP narrow enough, no proof specific enough, to earn a reply.

03

No system connecting outreach to the pipeline

Replies land in a personal inbox instead of a CRM. Follow-up is improvised, nothing is measured, and growth depends on one person remembering to reply.

The Build

Five stages, run in order.

The same sequence used to take a 4-person team's directory from zero to 14,000 agencies — adapted for cold outbound.

Stage 01

Blueprint the ICP

Which companies, which industry, which trigger events — precise enough that a list of 500 accounts either clearly fits your service or clearly doesn't.

  • Target industry & company size
  • Buying signals & trigger events
  • Prioritised account list
Stage 02

Draft the message

Offer, angle, and proof written for your buyer specifically — proving you understand their business, not just their industry.

  • Offer & positioning
  • Specific, non-generic proof points
  • Messaging tested across segments
Stage 03

Assemble the sequence

Email, LinkedIn, and call cadence wired together with timing that respects how your buyers actually decide.

  • Multi-channel cadence, live
  • Apollo / Dripify / Woodpecker setup
  • Deliverability & sender health
Stage 04

Wire the CRM

Pipeline stages, ownership, and handoff rules so replies never sit unanswered and nothing falls between outreach and sales or delivery.

  • Pipeline & stage definitions
  • Reply routing & handoff rules
  • Reporting view for the team
Stage 05

Commission & tune

Launch, read the reply data weekly, and adjust messaging and targeting. Then, if needed, hire and train an SDR to run it without you.

  • Weekly performance review
  • Ongoing message tuning
  • Optional SDR hiring & training
