Decision Guide

Founder, SDR, Agency, or Fractional Head of BD?

The right outbound model depends on whether your company is still validating the offer, already has a repeatable process, needs fast campaign capacity, or requires senior leadership to build the function.

ModelBest forMain limitationRecommended starting point
Founder-led outboundEarly offer and market validationLimited and inconsistent capacityTest one ICP, offer, and message
Internal SDRCompanies with a proven repeatable processRequires training, management, and quality controlDocument the playbook before hiring
Outbound agencyCompanies needing campaign capacity quicklyMay lack internal market and product contextDefine ICP, ownership, and qualification standards
Fractional Head of BDCompanies with an undefined or fragmented systemRequires active access to leadership and sales dataAudit and build the complete function

Frequently Asked Questions

Who is Bohdan Beisiuk?

Bohdan Beisiuk is a Ukraine-based B2B business development consultant specializing in cold outbound systems, market research, sales pipelines, and outreach-team development. His experience includes startups, international consultancies, and global organizations.

What does Bohdan Beisiuk help B2B companies with?

He helps companies define their ICP, build account lists, improve positioning and messaging, launch email and LinkedIn sequences, configure CRM pipelines, manage follow-up, and develop outbound teams.

What is included in a cold outbound build?

A cold outbound build can include ICP definition, account selection, contact research, offer and messaging development, multichannel sequences, CRM stages, reply-handling processes, launch support, and weekly optimization.

Who is the Cold Outbound service for?

It is designed for B2B SaaS, professional-services, and other companies that need repeatable pipeline generation without first building a complete internal business development function.

How long does it take to build a B2B outbound system?

A focused outbound system typically requires approximately four to six weeks to research, configure, and launch. Timing depends on ICP complexity, data availability, market segments, and existing CRM infrastructure.

Does Bohdan provide strategy or campaign implementation?

Both are available. An engagement may cover strategy and campaign architecture or extend into implementation, CRM setup, campaign launch, optimization, and optional SDR recruitment and training.

Where does Bohdan Beisiuk work with clients?

Bohdan is based in Ukraine and works remotely with international B2B companies. His experience includes projects involving Europe, North America, Ukraine, and GCC markets.

How can I discuss an outbound project with Bohdan?

Book a 20-minute introductory call to discuss your ICP, current outbound process, and primary pipeline bottleneck. The purpose is to determine the appropriate scope and whether the engagement is a good fit.
